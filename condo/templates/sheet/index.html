Google Sheet sync

The app can mirror the condo's transaction log in Google Sheets. Opening balances, monthly fees, payments, purchase credits and building expenses come from the sheet's Transactions tab; the Balances tab is used as a cross-check. Each sync replaces only what came from the sheet. Entries typed into the app by hand are kept.
